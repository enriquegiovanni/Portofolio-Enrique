About Project

Background

GOR Ratu Jaya is a badminton sports facility with multiple courts for public use. Before this system was introduced, court booking was done offline, requiring users to visit the location directly or call management to reserve a slot. This created challenges such as schedule conflicts, manual recording errors, and limited availability information.

This project was developed to digitize the badminton court booking process through a Laravel-based web application. Users can check court availability, select play dates and time slots, and complete bookings online without visiting in person. For administrators, the system streamlines reservations, court management, and scheduling.

Key Features

  • Online Court Booking — Online court reservation based on available dates and times.
  • Schedule Availability — Real-time court availability info to help users choose slots.
  • Booking Confirmation — Instant booking confirmation following verification.
  • Admin Dashboard — Comprehensive summary of bookings and court availability.
  • Booking Management — Administrative management of customer reservation records.
  • Court Management — Management of court information, pricing, and status.
  • Operating Hours — Configuration of operational opening and closing hours.
